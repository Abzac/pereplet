# Bundled Dmitri voice

The bundled `ru_RU-dmitri-medium` comes from rhasspy/piper-voices, revision
`e5349433ed883a32f893a434dc43a6c66b95ef7c`. File hashes are in model-manifest.json.
Pereplet distributes these model files unmodified. Training and conversion were
performed upstream; inclusion does not imply endorsement.

The Dmitri model card identifies the Russian recordings as CC0 and the training
base as en_US-hifi_tts_92-medium. Both MODEL_CARD and BASE_MODEL_CARD accompany
the voice. This revision replaces an earlier Lessac-based revision.

The base card credits Cori Samuel (reader 92), the Hi-Fi Multi-Speaker English
TTS Dataset, NVIDIA Corporation, and LibriVox source recordings. Dataset paper:
Bakhturina, Lavrukhin, Ginsburg and Zhang, Interspeech 2021.
Dataset: https://www.openslr.org/109/
Paper: https://arxiv.org/abs/2104.01497
Dataset license: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
The full CC BY 4.0 text is retained in supplemental/HiFi-TTS-CC-BY-4.0.txt.

These are dataset and training-chain notices. They must not be read as a new
license grant for model weights, or as a claim that GPLv3 covers those weights.
Upstream distinguishes these issues in:
https://github.com/OHF-Voice/piper1-gpl/blob/main/docs/LICENSING.md
https://github.com/OHF-Voice/piper1-gpl/blob/main/docs/COMMERCIAL_USE.md
An explicit distribution grant for the weights remains a release-review item.
Optional downloadable voices require their own review; they are not bundled.
